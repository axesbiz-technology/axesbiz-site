Our Story

From a small-town shop counter to a nationwide app

AxesBiz began in Farrukhabad, Uttar Pradesh, watching local shop owners juggle handwritten ledgers, WhatsApp reminders and spreadsheets just to know who owed them money. GST compliance made it harder still — most tools built for it were designed for accountants, not for the person actually running the counter.

So we built AxesBiz to work the way small business owners actually think: simple ledgers, one-tap invoices, and reports that make sense without a finance degree. Every feature in the app has been shaped by feedback from real shopkeepers, distributors and traders using it every day.

Today, AxesBiz is a complete business management suite — still built with that same small-counter simplicity at its core.
