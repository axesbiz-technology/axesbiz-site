Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AxesBiz ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application and website (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

We may collect: (a) account details you provide such as name, phone number, email, and business/GSTIN information; (b) business data you enter into the app, including invoices, ledgers, stock, and banking records; (c) device and usage information such as app version, device model, and crash logs; and (d) payment-related information processed by our third-party payment gateway (we do not store your full card or bank credentials).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to provide and improve the Service, generate invoices and reports, process payments, respond to support requests, send important account or service notifications, and maintain the security and integrity of the Service.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your business data is stored using industry-standard security practices, including encryption in transit. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse, though no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal or business data. We may share information with: trusted service providers who help us operate the Service (e.g. cloud hosting, payment processing); authorities where required by law; or with your consent. Any third-party payment processor handles your payment details under its own privacy policy.

5. Your Choices & Rights

You may access, update, or request deletion of your account data by contacting us at the email below. You may also uninstall the app at any time to stop further data collection from your device, subject to applicable record-retention requirements for financial data.

6. Data Retention

We retain your business and account data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal/tax record-keeping obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for business use by adults and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above, and continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact us at axesbiz.app@gmail.com or +91 7408096938.
